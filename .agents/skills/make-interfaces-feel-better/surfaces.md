# Surfaces

- Check text/icon combinations optically as well as geometrically. Adjust the affected Material Symbols glyph or spacing only when its visual weight looks off.
- Preserve the existing line and surface tokens. Keep structural separators; use a shadow only when elevation helps explain an overlay or interaction.
- If imagery disappears into its surrounding surface, consider an inset outline that does not change layout. Choose contrast from the existing palette and inspect both dark and bright images.
- Make small controls easy to target by enlarging their button or hit area without overlapping adjacent controls. Keep the visible focus indicator tied to the actionable element.
